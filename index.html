How do I order custom sizing?

Simply wrap a measuring tape or string around your wrist and share the measurement in cm or inches when inquiring. We calibrate the elastic fit accordingly.

How far in advance should I order for weddings?

For personalized Nikkah hoops, ceremony quills, and gift hampers, we recommend inquiring at least 10–14 days in advance to allow time for embroidery and safe delivery.
